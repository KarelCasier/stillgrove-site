Wordstrand privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Your save files stay on your device.

Wordstrand is made by Karel Casier, working as Stillgrove. This policy covers the Android game distributed through Google Play. There is no Wordstrand account or advertising.

The free campaign and already-owned packs work offline. Optional purchases and Restore purchases use Google Play and need a connection. Configured builds use Sentry for crash reports and GameAnalytics for gameplay statistics. Both settings start on when there is no previous choice; an existing Off choice stays off. You can turn either off separately in Settings, and continue playing.

Progress and settings

The game stores your progress, pack-ownership cache and settings in its private storage on your device. Guesses are processed locally. Wordstrand does not upload your save files or deliberately record your guesses in technical reports. Native crash data can include incidental information held in memory, as explained below. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Settings such as appearance, keyboard, clues, animation and sound are used to run the game as you choose.

Google Play purchases

Optional world packs are permanent purchases through Google Play when available. Google handles payment using your Google Play account. Wordstrand does not receive your card or bank details.

The app reads purchase records and uses purchase tokens to confirm and acknowledge purchases, or to restore pack access. It keeps only the confirmed pack-ownership flags in local storage. It does not save purchase tokens, order numbers, signatures or your Google account details in your game save.

Google Play and its billing software process purchase information and technical diagnostics under Google’s privacy policy. Google’s billing software includes a component that sends diagnostic information to Google’s logging service. Wordstrand does not pass your play progress, guesses or settings to that software.

Permissions and connections

  • Vibration: provides touch feedback during play and follows your phone’s touch-feedback setting.
  • Google Play billing: lets you buy and restore packs.
  • Internet and network state: support Google Play purchases, crash reports and gameplay analytics when enabled. Free levels and cached owned packs remain playable offline.

Wordstrand does not request access to your location, contacts, shared files, camera or microphone.

Diagnostics and bug reports

In builds configured for Crash reports, Wordstrand sends technical error and crash reports to Sentry, our diagnostic service provider, to help us find and fix problems. This setting starts on for new players and after an update from a version without a reporting choice. An existing Off choice stays off. You can turn Crash reports off in Settings, and the game works with reporting off. Builds without a configured reporting service do not send these reports.

When enabled, reports can include the game version, platform, operating-system and device details, technical error stacks, the kind of screen open and random technical session or installation identifiers. We do not add your name, email, advertising ID or a player profile. The network services involved receive connection information needed to deliver a report, such as an IP address; the game does not add your IP address to the report. Sentry’s project is configured to remove IP addresses and inferred geography from new events.

We disable log-file, screenshot and scene-tree attachments, local variables, source-code context and custom performance tracing. Gameplay statistics are handled separately, as described below. Ordinary error messages are replaced with fixed technical descriptions. Technical source and module paths may include an installation folder or OS username. We do not deliberately attach guesses, save files, email drafts or purchase tokens. A native crash report may nevertheless contain a snapshot of thread-stack memory, including incidental data present in memory at the crash. Script-level filtering cannot remove that data. We keep Sentry’s optional storage of raw minidumps disabled.

Turning Crash reports off stops new collection and removes queued reports from the app’s diagnostic cache. Reports already delivered or being transmitted cannot be recalled by that switch. Your choice is saved with the game’s normal settings; if a save fails, the app shows its retry notice. An older build without this setting does not use this Sentry integration.

Local recovery messages and diagnostic logs stay on your device. Google Play and Android may separately process crash and device diagnostics according to your device settings and Google’s privacy policy.

Report a bug opens your email app with a draft that includes platform and game-version information. Nothing is emailed to Stillgrove until you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Gameplay analytics

Configured builds use GameAnalytics to help us improve difficulty, pacing and hints. Gameplay analytics starts on for new players and versions without a previous analytics choice. A saved Off choice stays off. This switch is separate from Crash reports, and the game works with either or both off.

The game sends authored board identifiers, board starts and completions, numeric hint and guess-outcome counts, and bounded time spent in an attempt. It does not send the words you type, answers, clues, drafts, save files, purchase tokens or email contents.

The SDK adds a random installation identifier that persists across sessions, random session identifiers, game/build/platform/device and operating-system information, connection type, timestamps and session duration. We do not configure a personal account identifier or collect advertising identifiers. GameAnalytics receives the connection’s IP address and can use it to infer country; the app does not request location access. We disable automatic application-error reporting and health histograms, and do not send screenshots or replays. The SDK can still send fixed technical diagnostics about its own validation or storage failures when analytics is enabled.

When enabled, the SDK can queue events on your device while offline and submit them after reconnecting. Old telemetry can be dropped during extended offline use. Gameplay and saving do not depend on event delivery.

Turning Gameplay analytics off stops new gameplay collection and requests disabled SDK submission and local collection. It does not erase events already in the SDK’s queue or recall reports already delivered or in flight. A restart with saved Off does not initialize analytics or submit that queue. Turning it on again can send events cached before Off; actions while Off are not replayed by the game. After you switch it on, collection waits for the setting to be saved successfully, including a retry if saving fails.

Retention, deletion and security

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Doing so removes your local game data, including the ownership cache. Restore purchases can recover pack access through Google Play; it does not restore your played boards.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to respond, resolve the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing support@stillgrove.dev. There is no Wordstrand player account to delete.

Sentry error reports can be retained for up to 90 days during the initial service trial, then 30 days on the free Developer plan. We do not keep a separate archive of these reports. To request deletion, email support@stillgrove.dev with the approximate report date, game version and device model so we can try to locate the report. Because reports have no named player account, we may be unable to identify a particular report from an email address alone. Turning reporting off or uninstalling does not delete reports already delivered to Sentry.

GameAnalytics retention and available detail vary by event type and age under its published retention practices. A short dashboard lookback does not mean that all underlying data has been deleted. We do not export or keep a separate archive of these analytics. Contact support@stillgrove.dev about access or deletion; because the installation identifier is random, we may need information to identify your records. Switching analytics off or uninstalling does not remove previously delivered data.

Uninstalling does not delete Google’s purchase or diagnostic records. Google manages their retention and deletion under its own privacy policy and account controls.

Game files use Android’s private app storage. Purchases are handled by the Google Play service. Email is delivered by the email providers involved in your message. This policy page is served over HTTPS.

Children

The game does not create player accounts or ask players for personal details. Optional purchases are subject to Google Play’s account, payment and parental controls.

This policy page

This page has no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms. Its font and images are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub’s data handling.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game’s data practices change, and show the new date above. New reporting or analytics features will need corresponding disclosures before they ship.
