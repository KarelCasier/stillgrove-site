Wordstrand privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Your play progress stays on your device.

Wordstrand is made by Karel Casier, working as Stillgrove. This policy covers the Android game distributed through Google Play. There is no Wordstrand account, no advertising and no developer-operated gameplay analytics.

The free campaign and already-owned packs work offline. Optional purchases and Restore purchases use Google Play and need a connection.

Progress and settings

The game stores your progress, pack-ownership cache and settings in its private storage on your device. Guesses are processed locally. Wordstrand does not send your play progress, guesses or settings to Stillgrove or an analytics service. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Settings such as appearance, keyboard, clues, animation and sound are used to run the game as you choose.

Google Play purchases

Optional world packs are permanent purchases through Google Play when available. Google handles payment using your Google Play account. Wordstrand does not receive your card or bank details.

The app reads purchase records and uses purchase tokens to confirm and acknowledge purchases, or to restore pack access. It keeps only the confirmed pack-ownership flags in local storage. It does not save purchase tokens, order numbers, signatures or your Google account details in your game save.

Google Play and its billing software process purchase information and technical diagnostics under Google’s privacy policy. Google’s billing software includes a component that sends diagnostic information to Google’s logging service. Wordstrand does not pass your play progress, guesses or settings to that software.

Permissions and connections

  • Vibration: provides touch feedback during play and follows your phone’s touch-feedback setting.
  • Google Play billing: lets you buy and restore packs.
  • Internet and network state: are included with Google’s billing software. Free levels and cached owned packs remain playable offline.

Wordstrand does not request access to your location, contacts, shared files, camera or microphone.

Diagnostics and bug reports

Wordstrand currently has no developer-operated automatic crash-reporting service. Local recovery messages and diagnostic logs stay on your device. Google Play and Android may process crash and device diagnostics according to your device settings and Google’s privacy policy.

Report a bug opens your email app with a draft that includes platform and game-version information. Nothing is emailed to Stillgrove until you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Retention, deletion and security

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Doing so removes your local game data, including the ownership cache. Restore purchases can recover pack access through Google Play; it does not restore your played boards.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to respond, resolve the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing karel@stillgrove.dev. There is no Wordstrand player account to delete.

Uninstalling does not delete Google’s purchase or diagnostic records. Google manages their retention and deletion under its own privacy policy and account controls.

Game files use Android’s private app storage. Purchases are handled by the Google Play service. Email is delivered by the email providers involved in your message. This policy page is served over HTTPS.

Children

The game does not create player accounts or ask players for personal details. Optional purchases are subject to Google Play’s account, payment and parental controls.

This policy page

This page has no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms. Its font and images are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub’s data handling.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game’s data practices change, and show the new date above. New reporting or analytics features will need corresponding disclosures before they ship.
